1. Information we collect

Information created when you use the Service

  • Order history and status updates (for example, when an order was placed, prepared, sent out and delivered).
  • Account summaries such as your total number of orders and total spend, which help us offer loyalty promotions and customer support.
  • Your app settings, such as language (Burmese or English) and notification preferences.
  • Basic technical information needed for the app to work securely, such as device and app integrity checks and network status.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or your KBZPay / WavePay PIN or bank details.

2. How we use your information

  • To take and deliver orders — preparing your food, calculating delivery fees for your township, and giving your rider the name, phone number and address needed to deliver.
  • To process payments — matching your KBZPay, WavePay or Cash-on-Delivery payment to your order, and issuing refunds.
  • To keep you updated — order status notifications and in-app messages about your order.
  • To provide customer support — answering your messages, handling cancellations, returns and complaints.
  • To run promotions — applying promo codes and, where you have not opted out, telling you about offers.
  • To improve the Service — understanding which dishes are popular, fixing problems and planning stock.
  • To keep the Service safe — preventing fraud, fake orders and promo-code abuse, and protecting our customers, staff and riders.
  • To meet legal obligations — keeping business and tax records and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

3. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Our riders and delivery partners — your name, phone number, delivery address and order details, so they can deliver your order.
  • Payment providers — KBZPay and WavePay process mobile-wallet payments under their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers — companies that help us run the app, such as hosting, security and app-integrity services (for example, Google Firebase). They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Other customers — your name and the content of reviews you choose to post are visible to other app users.
  • Authorities — when required by law, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, staff or the public.
  • A new owner — if our business is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

4. Payments

When you pay with KBZPay or WavePay, you complete the payment inside the provider's own app. We receive only the information needed to confirm your payment (such as the amount, time and transaction reference). We never ask for, receive or store your wallet PIN, password or bank card details. For Cash on Delivery, we record only that payment is due and whether it has been collected.

6. Storage & security

  • Some of your information (such as your profile, cart and order history) is stored on your device so the app works quickly. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, this app data may be included in your device's backup under Google's terms.
  • Information needed to fulfil orders is also kept by us and accessed only by authorised staff who need it to do their jobs.
  • We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no app or internet transmission is completely secure, so please keep your password private and use a strong, unique password.
  • Our service providers may store or process data on servers located outside Myanmar. When this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information remains protected.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Order and payment records are kept for as long as needed for customer support, refunds and our legal, accounting and tax obligations.
  • Support messages and reviews are kept while they are useful for service and quality purposes, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Your choices & rights

You can:

  • view and update your name, phone number, email, township and address in the app;
  • ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information or your account (see below);
  • opt out of promotional messages at any time — we will still send messages about your orders;
  • turn off notifications in your phone settings.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity (for example, by contacting the phone number on your account) before acting on it. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account

To delete your account and associated personal information, contact us by phone, Viber, Telegram or email from the phone number or email address registered on your account and ask for "account deletion". We will delete your account and personal information within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law (such as transaction records), which we will keep only for as long as required. Reviews you posted will be deleted or made anonymous. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

10. Children

The Service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make an important change, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Please check this page regularly.
